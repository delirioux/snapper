# Snapper: quick cheat sheet

What every control does, by what you want to do. The [manual](MANUAL.md) goes through every page.

**Getting around:** KNOB 1 to 4 turn the four cards on the screen. A page's button opens it; pressed again, its
second page where it has one. HOME takes you back to PLAY. The keys only ever play music.

## Play

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start / stop | **PLAY**: with the hush (FORM on) |
| Tempo / volume | **SELECT** / **MASTER** |
| Another style | **PRESETS**: one of ten, on the next bar line |
| Another song | **ALGORITHM**: a new chord loop, bass line, riff and sounds in the same style, on the next bar |
| The big drums in | **REC** in the hush or rise: the tom fill and the break-in on the next downbeat; held, the tension holds until you let go |
| A fill | **REC** in the full band: a tom fill into a crash |
| Drama | **SEQ** from the full band: one held chord, a tom roll, the band back a key up |
| Play the keys part | **Keys**: snapped to the scale, anything you press is in key. **OCT-** / **OCT+**: their octave |

## The four knobs (PLAY)

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| The build | KNOB 1: **BUILD**, the hush and rise over 8, 16 or 32 bars, or HOLD (until REC) |
| Dramas | KNOB 2: **DRAMA**, AUTO or OFF (SEQ only) |
| Busier | KNOB 3: **ENERGY**, sixteenth hats, pickup kicks, a busier machine |
| The room | KNOB 4: **ROOM**, the drums' room |

## Sound

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| The parts' sounds | **FX**: **PADS**, **BASS**, **KEYS** (sixteen each), **DRUMS** (BIG, BOX) |
| Tone | **FX** again: **RESO**, **DRIVE**, **DETUNE**, **CRUSH** |
| Drums | **EDIT**: **KIT**, **KICK** (PUNCH, ROUND), **SNARE** (SNARE, CLAP, BOTH), **CRASH** |
| Levels | **GLO**: the four parts |
| Room | **GLO** again: **REVERB**, **DELAY**, **TIME**, **WIDTH** |

## The music

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Key and chords | **SCL**: **KEY**, **SCALE**, **CHORDS** (twelve loops), **BARS** a chord |
| Groove | **ARP**: **SWING**, **HATS**, **BASS** (PULSE, LONG, SYNCO, OCTAVE, SEQ, SLIDE), **FILLS** |
| The song's form | **ENV**: **FORM** (hush, rise, break-in, full, drama, end), **FULL** bars, **DRAMAS**, **KEY UP** a drama |
| The riff | **ENV** again (or **LFO**): **SPEED**, **RANGE**, **GATE**, **REPEAT** |

## Keep and connect

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Sets | **SAVE**: eight sets. **SET** picks, **LOAD** / **STORE** turned right, **CLEAR** turned right three times |
| Colour | Hold **HOME**: MENU, **COLOR**, **LOOK** |
| MIDI out | PADS on channel 1, BASS 2, KEYS 3, drums 10 |
| Saving | Automatic, while stopped |
