DRUMS: Snapper's drum kit, thirteen one-shots (00_kick, 01_kick_round, 02_snare, 03_clap, 04_hat, 05_open_hat,
06_crash, 07_tom_hi, 08_tom_mid, 09_tom_lo, 10_box_kick, 11_box_rim, 12_box_hat), synthesized by tools/sn_drums.py
(seeded, so it always makes the same files): no recording of any instrument and no third-party sample is in them.
The snare, the clap and the toms carry a synthetic gated room. 16-bit mono WAV at 44.1 kHz; tools/gen_samples.py
encodes them into the firmware.
To the extent possible under law, delirioux has waived all copyright and related rights to these sounds:
CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
PIANO: Snapper's piano, six notes (00_Piano_C2 .. 05_Piano_F#4, VCSL's names: C3 is middle C) of the Kawai
grand of the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL, by Sam Gossner and contributors, CC0 1.0,
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, "Chordophones/Zithers/Grand Piano, Kawai/Sustains", the v4 layer), changed by
tools/sn_piano.py: summed to mono, cut to 0.85 .. 1.15 s and faded, a high-pass at 180 Hz, a high shelf (+5 dB from 2 kHz), a presence
lift, less body, a soft clip, normalised. CC0 as their source; Snapper keeps it so.
Snapper uses none of Felucca's other CC0 sample sets (VCSL and VSCO-2 CE); tools/fetch_cc0.py still fetches them for
anyone who wants them back.
